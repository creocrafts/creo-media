Our story

Create fearlessly.

Our journey began with a simple goal: provide art lovers with high-quality, eco-friendly supplies that inspire imagination. Guided by a passion for creativity and sustainability, we carefully source materials that respect our planet.

Our marigold logo symbolises growth, warmth and renewal — values at the heart of every product we craft. A woman-owned brand designed in Illinois, USA, our FSC-certified, biodegradable papers blend global influences with timeless artistry.
